## 📶 **Wireless Networking Overview**

### 🔧 **Wireless Standards: IEEE 802.11**
- Managed by **IEEE** (Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers).
- The **802.11** family defines **Wi-Fi** standards.
- Certified by the **Wi-Fi Alliance** (look for the Wi-Fi logo on devices).
- Standards have both **technical names** (e.g., 802.11ac) and **user-friendly names** (e.g., Wi-Fi 5).

---

### 📊 **Major Wi-Fi Standards (802.11)**

| Wi-Fi Gen | Standard     | Frequencies Used       | Max Speed (Theoretical) |
|-----------|--------------|------------------------|--------------------------|
| Wi-Fi 1   | 802.11b      | 2.4 GHz                | 11 Mbps                 |
| Wi-Fi 2   | 802.11a      | 5 GHz                  | 54 Mbps                 |
| Wi-Fi 3   | 802.11g      | 2.4 GHz                | 54 Mbps                 |
| Wi-Fi 4   | 802.11n      | 2.4 GHz / 5 GHz        | 600 Mbps                |
| Wi-Fi 5   | 802.11ac     | 5 GHz                  | 6.9 Gbps                |
| Wi-Fi 6   | 802.11ax     | 2.4 GHz / 5 GHz        | 9.6 Gbps                |
| Wi-Fi 6E  | 802.11ax     | Adds **6 GHz** support | Similar to Wi-Fi 6      |
| Wi-Fi 7   | 802.11be     | 2.4 / 5 / 6 GHz        | **Up to 46 Gbps**       |

- Newer generations = faster speeds, better range, less interference.
- Speed also depends on antenna/radio configurations.

### 📱 **Cellular Wireless: 4G & 5G**

#### 📡 **4G / LTE (Long Term Evolution)**
- Unified GSM and CDMA standards.
- Download speeds:
  - **LTE**: ~150 Mbps
  - **LTE-A (Advanced)**: ~300 Mbps

#### 🚀 **5G**
- Introduced in **2020**.
- Huge performance increase.
- Speeds range from **100 Mbps to 10 Gbps** (theoretical).
- Enables:
  - More IoT devices
  - Real-time processing
  - Low latency (~1ms in ideal cases)
  - High-density environments (e.g., stadiums, cities)

---

### 🛰️ **Satellite Networking**

- Used in **remote locations** without traditional connectivity.
- Typical speeds:
  - **Download**: ~100 Mbps
  - **Upload**: ~5 Mbps
- **Latency**:
  - Traditional satellite: ~500 ms round trip
  - **Starlink (LEO)**: ~40 ms (goal: 20 ms)
- **Challenges**:
  - **Line of sight** required.
  - Weather-related issues (e.g., **rain fade**).
  - Higher cost and installation complexity.

---

### ✅ **Wireless Use Cases**

| Tech        | Best For                              |
|-------------|----------------------------------------|
| Wi-Fi       | Local wireless networks (home, office) |
| 4G / LTE     | Mobile internet for phones, tablets     |
| 5G          | High-speed mobile & IoT applications    |
| Satellite   | Remote areas without wired access       |
