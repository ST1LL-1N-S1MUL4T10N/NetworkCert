# 📡 Wireless Technologies – Network+ (N10-009) – Section 2.3

## 🔧 IEEE 802.11 Wireless Standards
- **802.11 Committee (IEEE)** sets worldwide standards for wireless networking.
- Standards are referred to by **Wi-Fi names** for simplicity:
  - **Wi-Fi 5**: 802.11ac
  - **Wi-Fi 6**: 802.11ax
  - **Wi-Fi 6E**: Extended version of 802.11ax
  - **Wi-Fi 7**: 802.11be
  - Future Wi-Fi versions: Wi-Fi 8, Wi-Fi 9, etc.

---

## 🌍 Wireless Frequencies & Channels
- **Common Frequencies**: 
  - **2.4 GHz**, **5 GHz**, **6 GHz**.
- **Channels**:
  - Frequencies are grouped into **channels** for ease of use (e.g., Channel 6, Channel 44).
  - **Bandwidths** can vary: **20 MHz**, **40 MHz**, **80 MHz**, and **160 MHz**.
- **2.4 GHz**: Limited to 3 non-overlapping channels (e.g., Channel 1, 6, 11).
- **5 GHz**: More channels, supports higher bandwidth (up to 160 MHz).
- **6 GHz**: Newer, more available channels.

---

## 📶 Wireless Bandwidth & Throughput
- **Bandwidth**: Range of frequencies available for transmission.
- Larger bandwidth = potentially higher **throughput** (data rate).
- **Channel width options**:
  - **20 MHz**: Standard, less interference.
  - **40 MHz**: Higher throughput, more interference.
  - **80 MHz** & **160 MHz**: Max throughput, higher risk of interference.

---

## 🏗️ Band Steering
- **Band Steering**: Directs devices to the optimal frequency band (e.g., 2.4 GHz vs. 5 GHz).
  - **Without band steering**: Devices automatically choose based on signal strength.
  - **With band steering**: Admin configures default band, e.g., 5 GHz.
  - Goal: Improve throughput and avoid congestion.

---

## 🌐 Global Wireless Regulations
- **FCC (Federal Communications Commission)**: Manages U.S. frequency allocations.
- **Global Cooperation**: Countries work together for consistent wireless management.
  - Example: **802.11h** adds interoperability features.
  - **DFS (Dynamic Frequency Selection)**: Automatically selects frequencies to avoid interference with existing networks.
  - **TPC (Transmit Power Control)**: Access points control power settings to optimize communication, avoiding conflicts.

---

## 📊 Key Features
- **DFS**: Access points automatically detect and avoid interference by selecting open frequencies.
- **TPC**: Controls transmit power, minimizing interference and optimizing performance.

---

## 💡 Key Takeaways
- **Wi-Fi Standards**: Wi-Fi 5 (802.11ac), Wi-Fi 6 (802.11ax), Wi-Fi 7 (802.11be).
- **Frequencies**: 2.4 GHz (limited), 5 GHz (more options), 6 GHz (newer).
- **Channels & Bandwidth**: More bandwidth = higher throughput but more interference.
- **Band Steering**: Optimizes frequency usage by steering devices to the best band.
- **Global Coordination**: Countries regulate frequencies for smooth wireless operation (DFS, TPC).
